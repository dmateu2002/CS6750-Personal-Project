How it works

Capture. Reflect. Recalibrate.

Coeta replaces open-loop tracking with a simple daily loop. Your reflections and your recovery quietly reshape next week's plan.

01 Capture

Do the work. Check off wins on the grid, or speak your raw, unfiltered thoughts straight into a private, on-device journal.

02 Reflect

Local intelligence reads your entries back to you — the trends, the blockers, the places you slipped. You grow by understanding your patterns, not ignoring them.

03 Recalibrate

Coeta reads your recovery. Strained days scale your targets down; strong days dial them up. Consistency, without the crash.

Questions

Good to know

What does "on-device intelligence" actually mean?
Unlike trackers that stream your journals to corporate servers, Coeta runs its intelligence directly on your phone using Apple Intelligence and local models. Summaries, pattern-finding, and goal scans are computed inside a secure local sandbox. Your personal text and entries are never sent to third-party cloud AI.
How does the recovery adjustment work?
When you enable it, Coeta reads Apple Health signals — HRV, sleep, resting heart rate — strictly on-device to estimate how recovered you are. On high-strain days it eases your goal load for the week so your consistency streak stays alive without pushing you into burnout.
Is Coeta really offline-first?
Yes. Local storage is the source of truth. Every change is written to your device first and syncs in the background only when you're online. The app works completely without a connection.

Your journals and board are also backed up to your own private iCloud, so you keep them when you change phones. That backup lives in your Apple account — we never receive it, and we cannot read it.
Why systems instead of goals?
Goals describe where you want to be. Systems are the daily loops that get you there. Most trackers reward far-off finish lines; Coeta tracks the next 24 hours. By measuring your consistency on the Win Board and learning from your reflections, you stop dreaming and start compounding.
